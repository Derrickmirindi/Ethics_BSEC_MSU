04 / Survey dimensions

Survey dimensions, their framework sources, and their relation to the Belmont principles

The thirteen rated items (Q3–Q15) combine the eight Stanford principles of ethical and effective service with five dimensions drawn from the CBPR literature. Each item is linked to the Belmont principle it most directly engages.

Respect for Persons

Treat individuals as autonomous agents and protect those with diminished autonomy.

6 items

Beneficence

Do no harm; maximize possible benefits and minimize possible harms.

6 items

Justice

Distribute the burdens and benefits of research fairly.

5 items

Some items engage two principles, so the counts add up to more than thirteen.
