Methods and governance

Use the standard responsibly

01

Co-design

Review wording, response scales, accessibility, and intended uses with residents, host sites, researchers, and program staff.

02

Consent and privacy

Explain who collects information, why it is needed, who can access it, how long it is retained, and how participants may withdraw.

03

Interpretation

Report response distributions and qualitative themes, not only averages. Protect privacy when examining small groups.

04

Action and closure

Return findings to communities, document decisions, assign owners and deadlines, and explain how recommendations were addressed.

Conceptual basis

This framework adapts principles of humility, respect and inclusion, reciprocity, preparation, safety and well-being, accountability, evaluation, and learning and reflection. These principles should be treated as interconnected, equally important, aspirational, and adaptable to context.
